🔬 Research Study Overview

Welcome to the Equiblue Agricultural Carbon Research Portal. This platform tracks paired field trial arms to evaluate long-term Soil Organic Carbon (SOC) sequestration, bulk density changes, and crop yield responsiveness.

PAIRED TRIAL ARMS DEFINITION:
• Case Field (Biochar): Treatment plot receiving biochar dosage.
• Control Field (Conventional): Parallel baseline plot under standard farming practices.
